Accessibility Notes

Contrast and usability considerations for the Yazaki palette.

Brand Primary Contrast Warning

The brand primary color #ed1c24 on a white background has a contrast ratio of approximately 4.0:1, which fails WCAG AA for normal-sized text (requires 4.5:1).

Recommendations:

  • Use the darker variant #a01018 (--brand-primary-dark) for text on white backgrounds.
  • Use white text on #ed1c24 backgrounds (passes AA at 4.0:1 for large text / bold 14px+).
  • Reserve #ed1c24 for large headings, icons, and decorative elements where AA Large applies.
